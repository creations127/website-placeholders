Our Story

A Family Legacy Built on Excellence

What began in 1959 with Tony Martinho learning his craft has grown into one of California's most respected tile companies. Tony started from a milk barn with all 11 of his children helping out — today, Visalia Ceramic Tile employs over 150 men and women across California, with second and third generation family members leading the way.

Our mission is simple: provide exceptional service and product to every customer, every time. We strive to surpass tile industry standards, just as Tony set out to do in 1959.

1959 Tony Martinho begins in the tile industry
1974 Obtains contractor's license as Martinho Ceramic Tile
1985 Visalia Ceramic Tile, Inc. established
2014 Named NTCA 5-Star Tile Contractor
2016 Largest employer of Certified Tile Installers in the U.S.

Natural Stone Surfaces

Find Your Perfect Stone

We specialize in custom solid surface countertops for residential kitchens, bathrooms, and commercial projects.

Engineered Quartz

Beauty without the upkeep.

As a manufactured countertop, quartz combines the look of natural stone with superior durability. Non-porous and stain-resistant, it won't harbor bacteria and never needs sealing.

  • Non-porous — resists stains from wine, tomato & coffee
  • No sealant required — low maintenance
  • Scratch & chip resistant
  • Chemical-resistant surface
  • Eco-friendly manufacturing
Get a Quartz Quote

Natural Granite

Each slab is a one-of-a-kind masterpiece.

100% natural granite means no two pieces are alike — every countertop carries a unique history formed over millions of years. Incredibly hard and durable, granite is one of nature's finest surfaces.

  • Second hardest natural material (behind diamond)
  • Heat resistant — place hot pans directly on surface
  • Scratch-resistant for everyday use
  • Increases home resale value
  • Unique natural patterning — no two pieces the same
Get a Granite Quote

Classic Marble

Timeless elegance, refined character.

Formed through heat and pressure over millennia, marble brings unmatched beauty with its signature heavy veining and wide color range. Best suited for bathrooms, vanities, and statement surfaces.

  • Distinctive veining unique to each slab
  • Takes a high polish for a mirror-like finish
  • Ideal for bathroom vanities and accent surfaces
  • Wide color range from pure white to deep grey
  • Timeless aesthetic that never goes out of style
